Beyond Amazon: Generating $98,727/Quarter on Walmart WFS and $89,293 on Newegg With Zero Promotional Discounting

For many e-commerce sellers, Amazon FBA represents 100% of their revenue. While Amazon is the largest retail engine in North America, relying on a single channel means leaving 40% to 60% of your addressable market untouched. Since 2018, Noma Enterprises Inc. has helped brands take their winning SKUs—and our own proprietary wholesale products—and syndicate them across Walmart WFS, Newegg, Best Buy, TikTok Shop, and Shopify.

1. Why Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS) Is the Best Expansion Move for FBA Sellers

Walmart.com attracts over 120 million unique monthly visitors in the United States, yet has roughly a fraction of the active third-party sellers competing on Amazon. That lower seller density translates directly into two advantages:

  • Lower Sponsored Search CPCs: Keyword bids on Walmart Connect are routinely 35%–50% lower than equivalent Amazon PPC placements.
  • Two-Day Shipping Buy-Box Dominance: By routing a portion of our sourced inventory into Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), listings automatically receive the "Fulfilled by Walmart" 2-day badge, which heavily weights the Buy Box in our favor even when competing sellers price lower.

In our managed Walmart portfolios, single bi-weekly statements regularly clear $40,587.62 in net payouts on $75,190.25 in product sales—while maintaining clean account health and $0.00 reserve holds on mature accounts.

2. Capturing $647–$977 AOV Orders on Newegg & Best Buy With 0% Promo Discounting

If you sell consumer electronics, computer hardware, office equipment, or smart-home gear—or if you stock high-ticket items from Noma’s own product catalog—generalist marketplaces aren't your only option. Specialty portals like Newegg (US, Canada, and Newegg Business B2B) and Best Buy Marketplace attract high-intent buyers and corporate IT departments.

Look at the Summary Table on the Right of the Screenshot: Out of $89,293.62 in Total Gross Revenue, Promotional Sales Revenue is $0.00 (0.00%). Because we source high-demand hardware SKUs with proper authorization, we don't need to slash margins with flash sales to move inventory.

3. How Our 7-Channel Syndication Workflow Operates

When you partner with Noma Enterprises Inc., you don't need to hire separate teams for Amazon, Walmart, Newegg, and Shopify. Our unified operations desk manages the entire multi-channel lifecycle:

  1. Centralized Sourcing & 3PL Allocation: Whether we hunt a custom product for your brand or allocate units from Noma’s own in-house inventory, stock arrives at our US/Canadian prep network and is split between Amazon FBA, Walmart WFS, and 3PL reserve.
  2. Channel-Specific SEO & Compliance: We optimize Listing Quality Scores (LQS) for Walmart, technical specification tables for Newegg & Best Buy, and viral creator affiliate briefs for TikTok Shop.
  3. Cross-Border North America Expansion: Winning US listings are immediately syndicated to Amazon Canada ($236K+ annual run rates), Amazon Mexico (MX$6.24M+), and Newegg Canada (CA$948 AOV).
