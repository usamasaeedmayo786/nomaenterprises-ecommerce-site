How We Scaled a US Amazon FBA Store to $1,893,608 Using High-AOV Product Hunting & Proprietary Sourcing

If you have ever hired an e-commerce agency or watched a typical Amazon FBA course, you have likely heard the same outdated advice: "Find a lightweight product under $25, source it cheaply on Alibaba, and turn on auto PPC." Since 2018, our team at Noma Enterprises Inc. has audited hundreds of struggling seller accounts—and that exact "$25 commodity trap" is the #1 reason sellers burn through their working capital.

1. Why the "$20 Impulse Item" Model Fails in Modern Amazon FBA

When we started managing marketplace stores in 2018, referral fees, inbound placement fees, and cost-per-click (CPC) bids were a fraction of what they are today. Back then, a $22 kitchen tool could absorb a $4.50 FBA fulfillment fee and a $0.65 click.

Today, if you sell a $22 item on Amazon FBA:

  • Amazon Referral Fee (15%): $3.30
  • FBA Pick, Pack & Inbound Placement Fee: $5.40 – $6.80
  • Landed Product Cost + Freight: $5.50
  • PPC Customer Acquisition Cost (10 clicks at $1.40 CPC): $14.00

Before a single return or storage fee hits your statement, you are already losing money on every unit sold. Yet 95% of freelance product hunters still pitch these exact low-AOV products because they only look at search volume—not net contribution margin.

2. Shifting to High-AOV ($140 to $659+) Product Hunting

Look closely at the verified Seller Central screenshot above. To generate $1,893,608.00 in Ordered Product Sales, our client did not need to ship 90,000 cheap units. We shipped 4,159 units at an Average Order Value (AOV) of $659.10.

Key Unit-Economics Insight: On a $659.10 FBA item, even if FBA fulfillment and shipping fees are $22 and you spend $35 in PPC to acquire the buyer, your fulfillment + ad cost is less than 8.7% of the retail price—leaving substantial net profit on every single order.

3. How Noma Executes All-Model Product Hunting & Sourcing

At Noma Enterprises Inc., we never force every client into the same rigid box. Depending on your capital tier and timeline, our procurement team executes three distinct sourcing models:

A. Direct Access to Noma’s Own In-House Product Catalog

Because we have operated continuously since 2018, we have spent over eight years developing our own proprietary product lines and tier-1 distributor allocations. Instead of waiting 90–120 days for custom tooling or cold-calling hundreds of wholesalers who reject new sellers, our partners can immediately stock proven SKUs directly from Noma’s own product inventory—complete with clean supply-chain invoices and Letters of Authorization (LOAs).

B. Authorized Brand Wholesale (FBA & Walmart WFS)

For investors seeking fast inventory velocity on established brands, our wholesale hunting desk scrubs distributor price lists of 5,000+ UPCs daily, filtering for listings with consistent Buy Box rotation, zero Amazon first-party dominance, and at least 18%–28% net ROI after all FBA fees.

C. High-Margin Private Label & OEM Factory Sourcing

When building a proprietary brand asset, we hunt categories where existing sellers have weak visual merchandising and 3.6-star review averages due to fixable engineering flaws. We negotiate directly with ISO-certified factories, manage pre-shipment quality inspections, and route container or air-freight shipments straight into Amazon FBA and 3PL warehouses.

4. Protecting Your IPI Score & Bi-Weekly Bank Payouts

Scaling top-line sales is pointless if your funds are locked in account reserves or eaten by aged-inventory surcharges. Our FBA operations team manages box-level inventory splits, barcode prep, and restock velocity so your Inventory Performance Index (IPI) stays healthy and bi-weekly settlements clear directly into your bank account.
